Jaca Social Publisher

Jaca Social Publisher is the first-party publishing tool operated by Global Foods Group / Healthy For Life Foods for the Jaca brand (allulose, a rare sugar). It schedules and publishes Jaca's own marketing images and videos to Jaca's own social media accounts, including the Jaca TikTok account.

What it does: on a recurring schedule, it posts approved Jaca brand creative to the brand's connected social accounts, and reads only the basic account info needed to publish. It does not collect data from the public and does not sell or share any data.
