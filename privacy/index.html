Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Jaca Social Publisher ("the app") is operated by Global Foods Group / Healthy For Life Foods ("we", "us") to publish the Jaca brand's own marketing content to the brand's own connected social media accounts.

Information we access

When an authorized Jaca account owner connects a social account, the app accesses only what is required to publish on that account: the basic account identity (account name and id) and the permission to create posts. For TikTok this corresponds to the user.info.basic and video.publish or video.upload scopes.

How we use it

We use this access solely to upload and publish the brand's own images and videos to the brand's own connected accounts on a schedule the brand controls. We do not use it for any other purpose.

What we do not do

We do not collect personal data from the public. We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties. We do not use the access for advertising profiling. We store only the access token needed to publish and the record of what we have posted.

Data retention and deletion

Access tokens and posting records are retained only while the integration is in use and are deleted when the connected account is disconnected or on request. To request deletion, contact info@jacasugar.com.

Contact

Global Foods Group, info@jacasugar.com. See also jacasugar.com privacy policy.
